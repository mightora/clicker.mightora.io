03 / BUILT FOR PEACE OF MIND

Simple to use.
Thoughtful by default.

Pairing links use strong random secrets. Only devices with the pairing link can send commands to your session. Sessions expire automatically after two hours, or you can end one immediately.

  • No registration or personal details
  • No presentation content is uploaded
  • Only the four arrow commands are relayed
  • Secure HTTPS and encrypted WebSocket connections

Network transparency

This online clicker must connect to Cloudflare to relay arrow commands between devices. The site also loads Mightora branding components from jsDelivr. We do not use advertising identifiers or store your commands after your session. To inspect requests, press F12 → Network, enable Preserve log and reload. Browser resource monitors cannot show all network activity.

Because it connects two devices in real time, a fully offline edition is not possible.

Open source and independently inspectable.
